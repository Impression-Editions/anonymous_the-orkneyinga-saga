About the Translator

Gilbert‌​‌​​‌‌‌​​‌‌​​‌‌‌‌​​‌​‌​​​​‌‌​‌​‌​‌​​‌​‌‌‌‌​​​‌​​​​​​​​‌‌​​‌​‌‌‌‌‌‌​​‌​​​‌‌‌‌​‌‌​​​‌​​‌​‌​​​​​‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌‌​‌​​​‌​​​​‌​‌‌​​‌​​‌‌‌​​​‌‌​‌​‌​​‌​‌​​‌‌‌​‌​‌​‌‌​​​‌‌‌‌​​‌​‌‌‌​‌​​‌​​​​‌‌‌​‌​​​​‌​‌​‌​​​​​​‌​‌​​‌‌‌‌​​‌‌​​​‌​​​​​‌‌​​​‌‌‌​‌‌‌​‌​‌​‌‌​​​​‌​‌‌​‌‌​​​​​​​ Goudie (1843⁠–⁠1918) and Jón A. Hjaltalín (1840⁠–⁠1908) were distinguished scholars whose collaborative efforts significantly advanced the study of Old Norse literature and Scottish history. Goudie, a Scottish antiquary and member of the Society of Antiquaries of Scotland, dedicated much of his life to documenting the historical heritage of the Northern Isles. Hjaltalín, an Icelandic scholar and librarian, brought an essential linguistic expertise to their partnership, bridging the gap between the medieval sagas and the English-speaking world.

Their definitive translation of The Orkneyinga Saga remains a cornerstone of historical scholarship, providing readers with an accessible yet rigorous account of the Norse earldom of Orkney. Beyond this seminal work, both men were prolific contributors to the academic discourse of their time, with Goudie’s extensive research into the charters and antiquities of Shetland and Hjaltalín’s contributions to Icelandic bibliography cementing their reputations as preeminent authorities in their respective fields. Their combined efforts in this volume continue to serve as an indispensable resource for historians and enthusiasts of Viking Age literature alike.
